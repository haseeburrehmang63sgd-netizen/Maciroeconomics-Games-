🧠 Cheatsheet: The two big rules

🌊 Floating exchange rate

  • Fiscal expansion → currency appreciation → net exports fall; output changes little under high capital mobility.
  • Monetary expansion → depreciation → net exports rise → output rises.
  • The exchange rate can move to help balance international flows.

⚓ Fixed exchange rate

  • Fiscal expansion is powerful: central-bank intervention expands money supply and supports output.
  • Independent monetary expansion is ineffective: the central bank reverses it to defend the peg.
  • Defending the peg may use foreign reserves during capital outflows.

These are standard textbook predictions for a small open economy. The strongest results assume high/perfect capital mobility and sticky prices in the short run; real-world outcomes can differ.
